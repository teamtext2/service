Website Services

1. Domain Name & Partner Solutions

  • Legally compliant registration: We support purchasing standard national and international domains (including .vn) through accredited registrar partners in full legal compliance.
  • Direct registrant ownership: The domain is registered directly under your name as the website owner — Spac2 never holds or retains ownership of your domain.
  • Annual domain renewal handling: We also assist in handling annual domain renewal payments so your website stays online effortlessly.
  • Partner support & VAT invoice issuance: Through our partner network, we issue official VAT invoices for a wide range of business service packages, including domain registration, Google Workspace, and more.

2. Website Interface & Web Applications

  • All website types & web applications: From corporate sites and e-commerce stores to custom web applications, every site we build supports installable web app (PWA) capabilities with extensive configuration options.
  • Limitless UI/UX design flexibility: Provide your own design samples (PNG, PDF, HTML demo, etc.), pick from pre-made templates, or let Spac2 design everything from scratch — virtually zero design limitations across any website category.
  • Sitemap & layout optimization: Provide your own sitemap or let our team structure and recommend the most effective layout for your business.

3. Exclusive Dedicated Admin Dashboard

  • Exclusive, isolated interface per website: No shared admin panels or generic templates. Each website receives its own custom-built Admin Dashboard tailored directly to its visual identity and architecture — completely separate from other clients.
  • All-in-one centralized control: Easily add or remove products, publish and edit articles, manage media assets, banners, menus, contact inquiries, and all system settings from one dedicated dashboard.
  • Very low add-on cost: This exclusive Admin Dashboard technology is available to clients for a minimal additional fee, giving you a dedicated management system without breaking your budget.
  • One-time payment — Zero monthly fees: Your website is engineered for a single one-time payment, running stably with no monthly subscription or hosting fees — you only pay your annual domain renewal fee.
  • Dedicated 1-on-1 consultation & custom modules: Contact our engineering team anytime for live admin previews, specialized workflows, and deep integrations tailored to your needs.

4. Structure & Edge Server Infrastructure

  • Edge Server & Global CDN architecture: Blazing-fast loading speeds, enterprise-grade DDoS protection, and virtually unlimited traffic, visits, and queries at zero server cost.
  • Speed verification before handover: Clients are welcome to test and benchmark live access speeds prior to final handover.
  • Your website can be installed as a standard Progressive Web App (PWA) and run Offline if required.
  • Full SEO & analytics support: Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, DNS guidelines for social media domain verification, and integrated traffic analytics.
  • Free Business Email included with domain registration: Complimentary business email system included when registering your domain with Spac2 (Business Email: view details here).

5. Handover, Ownership & Ongoing Support

  • Fast 3–7 day handover: Rapid development with complete handover in just 3 to 7 days, including full revisions tailored to your feedback.
  • We provide a standard Readme.md documentation file for easy maintenance and technical onboarding.
  • Ownership & Core worker.js Technology: You own your website interface, content, data, and domain outright with a single one-time payment. To ensure system security and safety for both Spac2 and our partners, Spac2 retains the core worker.js processing file.
  • Affordable post-handover edits & feature upgrades: Bug fixes are supported, and if you need design tweaks or advanced features beyond bug fixes later on, we are always ready to help for a tiny fee (the cost of a few cups of coffee) depending on complexity.
